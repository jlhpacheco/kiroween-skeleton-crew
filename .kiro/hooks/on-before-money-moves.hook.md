# Hook — On before money moves (both apps)
**Trigger:** any payment is about to move — a week's benefit (App 1) or a permit fee (App 2).
**Action:** Final check, in order: right person, right item, right amount from the rules, and not already paid for this period/permit.
**Never:** move money when any check fails. A second payment for the same week or permit is blocked, not warned about.
