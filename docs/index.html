Skeleton Crew

Kiroween 2026 — two small apps on one shared skeleton.

The idea, in one sentence

Form in → rules check → steps move → money moves → person sees a clear timeline.

Both apps use the same 5 steps. Only the form, the rules, and the words change.

  1. 1 Form in A person fills in a form.
  2. 2 Rules check Clear rules say yes or no.
  3. 3 Steps move The case moves step by step.
  4. 4 Money moves Money moves once. Never twice.
  5. 5 Timeline The person sees a clear timeline.

The two apps

App 1 — Unemployment claim

File a claim. Check the wages. Certify weeks. Get paid week by week.

Rules: California base period, CUIC §1280 weekly benefit table.

Open App 1

App 2 — Parking permits

Apply for a permit. Rules say yes or no. Pay the fee once — or pay $0 if exempt.

Rules: demo zones, demo fees, one permit per car per zone.

Open App 2
